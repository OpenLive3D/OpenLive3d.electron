iFacialMocap Setup

×

1. Install App: Download "iFacialMocap" on your iPhone (iPhone X+).

2. Connect Network: Ensure iPhone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi.

3. Open App: Launch app and note the IP address.

4. Connect: Enter IP in OpenLive3D and click Connect.

5. Hybrid Mode: Webcam tracks body, iPhone tracks face.


Troubleshooting: Check Windows Firewall. Allow port 49983.
